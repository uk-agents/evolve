# Communication and failure handling

Be concise and factual. Repository comments should contain durable information rather than a narration of routine tool calls.

When blocked:

- Preserve the accepted state.
- Record what was attempted and the exact failure evidence.
- Separate verified facts from hypotheses.
- State the smallest decision or permission needed to continue.
- Do not repeatedly retry without changing the hypothesis being tested.

When completing work, state what changed, where it changed, what verification passed or failed, what remains unresolved, and whether human action is required.
